Visiting Philadelphia

Getting here, getting around, and what to do
with the rest of the weekend.

Getting there

The party is at 1220 North Front Street, in Fishtown.

Transit
The Front–Girard stop on the L is at the corner — ten minutes from Center City. The 15 trolley and buses 5 and 25 stop nearby.
Driving
Street parking only, and it fills up on a Friday — carpool or take transit if you can.
Remember
Be inside by 7:15 PM. If you'll be later than that, come after 8:00 PM.

Paying for transit

No ticket needed — tap a contactless card or your phone as you board. Rides are about $2.90; one card can pay for up to five people. From the airport, the Airport Line reaches Center City in 25 minutes for $5.

Our guide to the city

Evan keeps a running guide to the city — what's on, where to eat, and more.

Open the guide

Worth your time

  • Fishtown & Northern Liberties — where the party is. Bars, music venues and pizza.
  • Old City — Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell, cobblestones. Walkable from Fishtown.
  • Rittenhouse & Center City — downtown, and the smartest restaurants.
  • East Passyunk — South Philly, the Italian Market, the best value eating in the city.
  • The Parkway museums — the Art Museum steps, the Barnes, the Rodin.

Good to know

  • It's a grid, and a small one — Center City is a half-hour walk end to end.
  • September evenings turn cool and there's outdoor space — bring a layer.
  • BYOB is a Philadelphia institution — plenty of restaurants are BYOB too.
